2. Project Background
The objective of this project is to develop a modern, professional and mobile-first official website representing Shri Jitendra Kumar Yadav's public profile, social-service activities, public initiatives, achievements, news, events and photo/video gallery.
The website will serve as a central digital information platform where citizens and visitors can easily access verified information regarding:
	•	Public profile and biography
	•	Current responsibilities
	•	Public-service initiatives
	•	Social welfare activities
	•	Development-related work
	•	News and updates
	•	Events and programmes
	•	Photo & video gallery
	•	Achievements
	•	Public contact information
	•	Important announcements
The platform should establish a strong and trustworthy digital presence and make publicly available information easier to discover through search engines.
3. Project Objectives
Primary Objectives
	•	Create a professional official digital identity.
	•	Showcase public-service and community-development activities.
	•	Maintain a transparent record of important initiatives and achievements.
	•	Publish verified news and activity updates.
	•	Provide an organized photo/video gallery.
	•	Make the website easily accessible on mobile devices.
	•	Implement SEO so legitimate public information can be easily discovered through Google and other search engines.
	•	Provide an easy-to-use CMS for regular updates.
	•	Create a scalable platform that can accommodate future features.
